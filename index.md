# Privacy Policy for BudgetFam

**Effective date:** November 8, 2026

BudgetFam ("the app," "we," "us") is developed by Princewill Ohuabunwa, an individual developer. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices regarding that information.

BudgetFam is currently distributed privately (TestFlight / internal testing tracks only) for use by a limited set of households, not through a public app store listing.

## Information We Collect

**Account information:** when you sign up, we collect your email address and a password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password, only a securely hashed version it manages). You also provide a display name.

**Household and budgeting data:** to provide the app's core functionality, we store the information you and other members of your household enter, including:

- Household name and membership (who's in your household, and who invited whom)
- Income sources
- Budget categories and budgeted amounts
- Transactions (amount, date, description, category)

This data is scoped to your household — only members of the same household can see it, and some categories can be marked private to a single member.

**Diagnostic information:** when something goes wrong in the app, we may record an error log (what failed, when, and which account/household it happened under) to help us fix bugs. We don't use this for any purpose beyond troubleshooting.

**What we don't collect:** BudgetFam does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, does not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes, and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide the app's functionality: authenticating you, syncing your household's budget data across devices and members, and diagnosing technical problems when they occur.

## Where Your Information Is Stored

Your information is stored on servers operated by our database provider, Supabase, located in the United States. Session credentials are stored encrypted on your device (via your device's secure storage, e.g. Keychain on iOS) rather than in plain text.

## Your Choices

**Deleting your account:** you can permanently delete your account at any time from the app (More → Profile → Delete my account). This removes your sign-in permanently. If you're the last remaining member of a household, deleting your account also permanently deletes that household and everything in it. If other members remain in your household, your past transactions and budget entries stay attached to the household for those other members' records, but your own sign-in and access are gone for good and cannot be recovered.

**Access or correction requests:** for anything the in-app deletion flow doesn't cover — for example, requesting a copy of your data, or correcting information you can't edit yourself — contact us at the email below.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the effective date above when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
